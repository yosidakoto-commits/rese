Business 06

International Trade & Overseas Expansion Business

Our international trade and overseas expansion business brings Japanese regional products and traditional-industry goods to overseas markets, centered on Greater China (mainland China, Hong Kong and Taiwan). Drawing on our Greater China network and business base, we handle the full operation of overseas expansion — connecting with local importers and distributors, building sales channels, and communicating to local audiences — so that producers can focus on supplying their products. We support the overseas expansion of locally rooted products such as sake and other alcoholic beverages, foods and crafts.
