Business 03

Traditional & Vacant House Revitalization Business

Japan has roughly nine million vacant houses and traditional homes. We bring them back to life for modern uses while preserving their cultural value — as accommodation (traditional house hotels), coworking spaces, and community hubs — with end-to-end support from planning to construction and operation, tailored to local needs and each property's character.
