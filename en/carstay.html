Business 01

Vehicle Overnight Infrastructure Business

We design, develop, and operate next-generation mobility rest hubs for the rapidly growing van-life and vehicle-overnight population. RESE's original vehicle overnight spots — with free solar-powered charging stands, Wi-Fi, and showers — are deployed on idle land, at roadside stations, and on traditional house grounds across Japan, creating stay-style exchange hubs that connect travelers and communities.
